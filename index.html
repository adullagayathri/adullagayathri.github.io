About

Systems thinking, applied to vision and language

I'm an AI Engineer with hands-on experience building computer vision, multimodal AI, and machine learning systems that solve real-world problems — spanning research, internships, and production deployments that turn large volumes of image, video, and structured data into scalable AI solutions.

At Third Estate Analytics, I build computer vision pipelines that analyze dashcam imagery, detect housing code violations, and automate address-level reporting for municipal stakeholders. I joined the project through a University at Buffalo industry collaboration and was invited to continue as an AI Engineer after successfully delivering the initial system.

Previously, I worked on distributed data engineering, feature pipelines, and ML infrastructure during internships at TresVista and ZS Associates, and as a Graduate Research Assistant at the University at Buffalo, where I developed 3D human reconstruction and rendering pipelines using SMPL-X and PyTorch.

My technical interests sit at the intersection of computer vision, multimodal & vision-language models, generative modeling, and ML infrastructure — built with Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow, Docker, Kubernetes, Spark, FastAPI, Redis, AWS, and GCP.

I'm currently seeking full-time AI Engineer / Machine Learning Engineer roles where I can build scalable, production-grade AI systems.
